
Data from:
"Nicholas C Wu, Lei Dai, C Anders Olson, James O Lloyd-Smith, Ren Sun (2016) Adaptation in protein fitness landscapes is facilitated by indirect paths eLife 5:e16965."
https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.16965 

The data can be found at:
https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.16965.024 , and
https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.16965.025
where it is licensed CC-BY 4.0

The data in `elife-16965-supp1-v4.xlsx` and `elife-16965-supp2-v4.xlsx` is provided as-is from elifesciences.org, 
while `wu_2016_1.csv` and `wu_2016_2.csv` is the same data in CSV format.

The sequences (first column) are 4 amino acids (sites 39, 40, 41, and 54) from protein G domain B1. The fitness values (fifth column) are the relative binding frequency of each varient to immobilized immunoglobulin gamma (IgG-FC), normalized to the binding of the wildtype. Varients missing from `elife-16965-supp1-v4.xlsx` are present in `elife-16965-supp2-v4.xlsx`, where the fitness values are estimated by regression.
