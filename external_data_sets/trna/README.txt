
Data from:
"Domingo, J., Diss, G. & Lehner, B. Pairwise and higher-order genetic interactions during the evolution of a tRNA. Nature 558, 117–121 (2018)."
https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-018-0170-7

The data can be found at: https://static-content.springer.com/esm/art%3A10.1038%2Fs41586-018-0170-7/MediaObjects/41586_2018_170_MOESM2_ESM.xlsx
Reused with permission from Ben Lehner.

The data in `41586_2018_170_MOESM2_ESM.xlsx` is provided as-is from static-content.springer.com, 
while `domingo_2018.tsv` is the same data in TSV format.

The sequences (first column) are the 73 nucleotides of the arginine tRNA in Saccharomyces cerevisiae (yeast), of which positions 1, 2, 6, 27, 43, 46, 66, 69, 70, and 71 were mutated. The fitness values (fourteenth column) are the logarithm of fitness of each varient grown, normalized to the fitness of the wildtype (first row). Fitness values were averaged across 6 replicate experiments.
